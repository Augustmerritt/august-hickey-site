About

August Hickey is a Missoula, MT-based saxophonist playing weddings, food and beverage venues, and private events. Sets lean on tenor and alto sax with a genuine jazz sensibility — melodic, flexible, and built to fit the room.

August also teaches saxophone to students from complete beginners to advancing players, of all ages, both in person and online.
